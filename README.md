# Mungana_RD
Some data science studies to assess the Cu mineralisation at Mungana and Red Dome (Chillagoe, NE QLD)


1. The limitations of a standard Plotly 4D plot to assess the Cu mineralisation are shown. Nonetheless it can quickly illustrate the presence of Cu mineralisations with As well below the standards safety limit (2000 ppm).

2. A PCA and PCA biplot (scikit-learn) is to be presented on the data points with Cu >= 1% (typical economic grade) to evaluate the control factor of high grade Cu / low As mineralisation.
