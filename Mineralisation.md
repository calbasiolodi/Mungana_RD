# Mineralisation characteristics and mode of occurrence

The Cu mineralisation occurs in different modes according to the mineralised zone, here the different modes of occurence in the host rock are described from drillcore RD 997

## Cu in chalcopyrite and bornite mineralisation within marble (e.g. -425 m in RD 997 drill core)
This mineralisation is lower grade about 3% Cu, with virtually 100% of Cu minerals being either millimetric grains within a marble matrix. Despite the low grade
it is the easiest to process since gangue is monomineralic (marble) and the bulk As content is well below the safe limits (2000 ppm).
